Change the board — numbering sorts itself out

Insert, delete, or drag a shot, and everything downstream — numbers, timecodes, schedule assignments — follows automatically. You edit the content; STB keeps the structure in sync. Linked shots are grouped as 05-1 / 05-2 and move as one, however you rearrange them.

Sketch

Pick up the pencil and draw right in the frame

On iPad, tap a storyboard frame with Apple Pencil; on Mac, hover over the thumbnail and click ✏️. Either way you land in the sketch editor — pen, marker, eraser, undo. Nothing to learn. And the positioning is honest: it doesn't compete with a storyboard artist on polish — it competes with "no storyboard" on clarity.

・Scene and character layers — draw composition on the scene layer, performance and camera moves on the character layer. Duplicate a shot, clear just the character layer, redraw — same scene, new action, one shot a minute.
・Trace over scout photos — open sketch on a frame that has a photo and it dims to a semi-transparent underlay you can trace, so the composition is right from the start; the output is always a clean line drawing.
・Two-finger tap = undo, three-finger tap = redo (works app-wide); palm rejection means resting your hand on the screen leaves no marks.
・Sketches are saved in the project, so you can return to the editor and keep working anytime; presentation and export treat sketches exactly like any other storyboard frame.

iPad

iPad: gestures are the whole interface

Same project, same screens — the controls just switch to touch. One list to remember:

・Apple Pencil tap on a frame = straight into sketch; finger tap = pick a photo (native photo picker; originals stored in iCloud finish downloading before they come in)
・Finger-drag a handle (⠿, the SHOT header, the time column in the shooting schedule) = reorder — the card follows your finger and snaps back if you drop it nowhere
・Long-press a shot card = multi-select: tap cards to select/deselect, press "Done" to finish → link shots, assign to a block, batch delete
・Two-finger tap = undo, three-finger tap = redo — app-wide, including inside the sketch editor
・Projects live in Files ▸ On My iPad ▸ STB — back up, rename, or delete by acting on the whole project folder; AirDrop the folder to move a project to another device
・Export = the native share sheet — AirDrop the PDF/PPTX to a Mac, save to Files, or message it straight to your producer

Not on iPad yet (in development): video import and SVG logo replacement — do those on the Mac when needed; the same project works on both.
